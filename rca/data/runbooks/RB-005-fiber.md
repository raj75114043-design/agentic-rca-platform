# RB-005 Backhaul Link Down

## Symptoms
Open LINK_DOWN incident; OTDR trace shows a break at a measured distance from the site.

## Diagnosis
1. Use the OTDR distance to locate the break along the fibre route.
2. Check for third-party civil works near the route and the fibre activity that laid the cable.

## Resolution
- Dispatch the fibre splicing team to the break location.
- Reroute traffic over microwave backup where available.
- Log a damage claim if the cut was caused by a third party.

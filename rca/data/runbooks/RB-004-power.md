# RB-004 Site Down due to Mains Power Failure

## Symptoms
Open POWER_FAIL incident; the site is DOWN; batteries exhausted.

## Diagnosis
1. Check for an active regional event such as GRID_OUTAGE in the site's region.
2. Count other sites in the region with POWER_FAIL incidents to confirm a regional cause.

## Resolution
- Dispatch portable diesel generators to priority sites.
- Coordinate restoration timelines with the state utility and inform the NOC.
- Review battery autonomy; sites below 4 hours should receive battery upgrades.

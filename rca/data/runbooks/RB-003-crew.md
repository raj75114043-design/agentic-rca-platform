# RB-003 Crew Not Mobilised

## Symptoms
An activity is blocked with reason "Crew not mobilised" and the assigned crew is marked unavailable.

## Diagnosis
1. Check the crew's availability and unavailable reason.
2. Look for another available crew of the same team in the region.

## Resolution
- Reassign the activity to an available crew in the same region and team.
- If no crew is available, request a contractor crew through the rollout PMO.

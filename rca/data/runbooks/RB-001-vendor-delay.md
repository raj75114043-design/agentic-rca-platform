# RB-001 Material Delivery Delay

## Symptoms
An activity is IN_PROGRESS with blocked reason "Awaiting material" and a purchase order is overdue against its promised date.

## Diagnosis
1. List purchase orders for the blocked activity and identify those with no delivery date past the promised date.
2. Check the vendor scorecard for the on-time rate and other open overdue orders.
3. Use the dependency graph to find other sites that depend on the same vendor.

## Resolution
- Escalate to the vendor account manager when an order is more than 10 days overdue.
- Request an expedited dispatch or partial shipment; consider an alternate approved vendor for the material.
- Update the site's planned on-air date once a committed delivery date is received.

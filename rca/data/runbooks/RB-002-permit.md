# RB-002 Permit Pending

## Symptoms
Civil works have not started; blocked reason "Site access not permitted"; the municipal permit status is PENDING past its expected date.

## Diagnosis
1. Check the permit authority, application date and expected approval date.
2. Confirm no civil activity has a completion date.

## Resolution
- Escalate through the regional liaison officer with the municipal authority.
- Prepare a no-objection letter from the landlord and resubmit missing documents.
- Re-plan crews to other sites until approval is granted.
